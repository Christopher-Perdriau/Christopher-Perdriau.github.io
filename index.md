I'm a CODA (Child of Deaf Adults) and native ASL user, and a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (advisor: Colleen M. Lewis) — I defended in April 2026, with my degree formally conferred in December 2026. My research is in CS education and accessibility — I study and design for the experiences of Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing students in computing courses, and more broadly how CS classrooms and hiring pipelines include or exclude students. Growing up in the Deaf community motivates and positions me to do this work. I hold a B.S. in Computer Science (minor in Psychology) from Oregon State University, where I worked with Margaret Burnett on inclusive software design (GenderMag).

Alongside research, I've also worked as a software engineer, most recently building a client-side analytics feature for a multifamily energy-use dashboard (Svelte / NestJS / AWS) — see the [Projects](/projects/) page. I'm on the market for research-track academic positions (faculty, postdoc, research scientist), applied/UX qualitative research roles — especially in accessibility and Deaf-community-centered contexts — and industry roles in software engineering and applied research.
